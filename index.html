Exterior wash

A straightforward exterior clean with attention to the parts that make the biggest difference.

Hand wash

We wash the whole outside of your car by hand, working carefully around the paintwork, windows and trim, then rinse it all off thoroughly.

Wheels

Wheels get their own proper clean, so dirt and brake dust are scrubbed out of the rims and between the spokes.

Tyres

We clean the tyre walls and wheel arches, so the whole wheel area looks fresh instead of muddy.

Drying

We dry the car by hand to help avoid water marks, leaving a clean finish on the paint and glass.

Small cars and SUVs only. We do not currently wash large vans. Prices vary depending on the job, so message us for a quote.
